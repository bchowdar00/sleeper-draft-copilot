### RB tier boundaries (what defines the cliff)
- **Tier 1 (ADP 1.5–2.3): true bell cows with receiving volume, zero competition.** No cliff inside; the cliff is right after — pick 3 is a full round-plus later.
- **Tier 2 (6.6–10.3): elite but each has a real question mark** (McCaffrey age/injury, Achane offense). Draft urgency: moderate — these go fast in Rd 1.
- **Tier 3 (12.5–19.9): high-floor RB1s, several with contract/role swing factors resolved in their favor.** Cliff after 19.9→21.1 is small; real cliff is the jump from Tier 3 into unresolved backfields at Tier 4.
- **Tier 4 (21.1–33.7): talented but each carries a flag** (new team fit, rookie/injury, suspension risk, unproven full-season role). This is the tier where our disagreement edge matters most — see §3.
- **Tiers 5–6 (36.7–52.4): locked-in starters without top-24 ceiling.** Safe floor plays.
- **Tier 7 (57.7–69.4): committee backs with real weekly variance.** Urgency spikes here — these disappear fast because handcuff/committee value is inconsistently priced across platforms.
- **Tier 8 (73.5–98): the "one injury away" tier** — several ClutchPoints undervalued/FantasyPros riser calls cluster here (Dowdle, Brooks, Harvey).
- **Tiers 9–10 (104.7+): touchdown-dependent or pure-handcuff dart throws.** No meaningful in-tier cliffs; draft for roster construction/upside, not floor.
