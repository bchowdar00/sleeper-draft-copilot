## 3. Tier Boundaries and Cliffs (QB & TE)

**QB cliffs (per NFL.com tiers + ADP gaps):**
- **Tier 1 → 2**: Josh Allen alone in Tier 1 (ADP 35); a real gap to Tier 2 starting at Lamar Jackson (ADP 51-58). Allen is the only QB going in rounds 2-3; the rest of the "elite" group goes rounds 4-6.
- **Tier 2 → 3 cliff (the important one)**: Tier 2 ends at Jayden Daniels (ADP 72); Tier 3 begins at Dak Prescott (ADP 69) through Kyler Murray (ADP 143). This is a soft cliff — ADP overlaps at the boundary — but points-per-game separation is real: Tier 2 QBs combine rushing floor + high-end passing, Tier 3 are passing-only or timeshare-rushing.
- **The late-round QB cliff**: There is a real dead zone from roughly ADP 160 (Jordan Love) to ADP 195+ (Stroud) where startable-if-necessary QBs vanish — Tiers 4/5 blur together. This is the range where the "wait on QB" strategy pays off: Bo Nix (ADP 118) and Jaxson Dart (ADP 124) are the last true weekly-starter QBs before a real dropoff; after Kyler Murray (143) the position gets streamable/matchup-only until the rookie QB tier (Cam Ward 251, Mendoza 228 as backup).
- **Superflex changes this entirely** (see Section 1) — the late-round-QB dead zone disappears because backups (Watson, Cousins, Brissett) are drafted as speculative QB2/3s.

**TE cliffs (per Footballguys/PFF tiers + ADP):**
- **The "elite TE" cliff**: McBride/Bowers (ADP 29-37) form a clear Tier 1, going in rounds 3. Tier 2 — Loveland, Warren (ADP 56-68) — is still a round-earlier-than-TE-replacement-level premium, but PPG separation from Tier 1 is real (roughly 3-4 PPG per Footballguys' narrative).
- **The big TE cliff sits between Tier 2 (ends ~ADP 68, Warren) and Tier 3 (starts ~ADP 79, Pitts)** — about an 11-pick gap where almost no TE goes off the board, meaning if you miss the first 4 TEs you should not reach for TE5-7 (Pitts/Fannin/Kraft, ADP 79-104) at a premium; wait further.
- **Streaming threshold**: From LaPorta (ADP 123) downward through the rest of Tier 4 (roughly ADP 130-230), weekly PPG differences are small — this is the "stream TE off matchups" zone, mirroring the standard late-TE strategy advice. The gap between Tier 3 and Tier 4 is the second real cliff (~ADP 108→123 is fine, but 130+ flattens hard).
- Net: the "elite TE" argument in 2026 is strongest for McBride/Bowers only (clear #1 tier); Loveland/Warren are "TE1.5" — good value, not true elites; everything from Pitts on down is fungible enough to punt.
